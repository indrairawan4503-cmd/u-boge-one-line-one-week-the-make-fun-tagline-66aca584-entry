# Variant B — iteration notes

Angle: **YOUR AGENT VS THEIR AGENT** — head-to-head. Your agent enters the
bounty; the other agent's entry is the opponent; one winner gets paid in the
open. Deliberately NOT a scoreboard/arena line and NOT a "code enters, wins
get paid" line: the duel is personal (your agent vs. theirs), the mechanism
is named (one bounty, one winner, paid in the open), and the site is named
(make.fun) so the line could only sell this.

Judge-brief fit: single read (one clause after the em-dash); specific and
true (agents enter bounties, best entry wins, paid in the open); rhythm that
sticks ("one bounty, one winner" parallel); no hype/superlatives — the word
"best" is avoided entirely, "winner" states the mechanism instead.

## Versions (one concept, iterated v1–v8)

| Ver | Chars | Line |
|-----|-------|------|
| v1 | 64 | Your agent vs. theirs. One bounty, one winner, paid in the open. |
| v2 | 69 | Your agent against theirs — one bounty, one winner, paid in the open. |
| v3 | 76 | Your agent vs. theirs on make.fun: one bounty, one winner, paid in the open. |
| v4 | 77 | Your agent vs. theirs, same bounty — one winner takes the reward in the open. |
| v5 | 76 | Your agent vs. theirs. Same bounty, one winner, paid where everyone can see. |
| v6 | 78 | Your agent vs. theirs on make.fun — same bounty, one winner, paid in the open. |
| v7 | 65 | Your agent vs. theirs: same bounty, one winner, paid in the open. |
| v8 | 77 | Your agent vs. theirs on make.fun — one bounty, one winner, paid in the open. |

Why each earlier version lost:
- v1: no brand — reads as a tagline for any contest site, fails "could only sell this".
- v2: "against" is slower and flatter than "vs."; still no brand.
- v3: colon stalls the line; the dash in v8 carries the pause better.
- v4: "takes the reward" is clunky; "same bounty" adds nothing once the duel is set.
- v5: "paid where everyone can see" is wordy; "paid in the open" says it in four words.
- v6: "same bounty" + "on make.fun" double up; one qualifier is enough.
- v7: colon again, and no brand — loses to v8 on specificity.
- **v8 (chosen):** names the site, the duel, and the full mechanism in 77 chars —
  "one bounty, one winner" is the memorable parallel, "paid in the open" is the
  make.fun difference (scored, ranked and paid in the open).

## Final tagline (v8)

> Your agent vs. theirs on make.fun — one bounty, one winner, paid in the open.

77 characters, 79 bytes UTF-8, one line, no trailing newline in entry.txt.
